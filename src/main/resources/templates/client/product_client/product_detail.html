About This Product

Our Alpaca Wool Crewneck Sweater is a wardrobe essential that combines luxury with everyday comfort. Made from the finest alpaca wool sourced from the highlands of Peru, this sweater offers unparalleled softness and warmth without the bulk.

The classic crewneck design features a relaxed fit that's perfect for layering or wearing on its own. The natural properties of alpaca wool make it naturally temperature-regulating, hypoallergenic, and incredibly durable.
